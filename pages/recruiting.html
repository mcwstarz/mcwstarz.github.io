Our Process
Three Steps to Recruiting Clarity

A proven framework to move from confusion to confidence.

Snapshot Assessment

We evaluate where your athlete stands right now.

  • Graduation year & timeline
  • Academic profile
  • Position & skill set
  • Current exposure level
  • Communication history

Where does your athlete stand on potential college recruiting boards right now?

Honest Level & Fit Evaluation

We assess realistic opportunities based on the full picture.

  • DI / DII / DIII projection
  • Film review & skill assessment
  • Academic alignment
  • Realistic opportunity conversation

No hype. No false promises. Just positioning.

Strategic Roadmap

We build a plan that turns goals into action.

  • Target school list
  • Communication strategy
  • Exposure alignment
  • Early Decision strategy
  • Timeline & milestones

Recruiting becomes intentional instead of reactive.
