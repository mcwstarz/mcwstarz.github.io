Our Mission
Changing Lives Through Basketball

MCW Starz is committed to serving those less fortunate by offering scholarships to talented and aspiring athletes who may otherwise not be able to participate in an elite AAU program.

Over 8 years, we've placed over 120 players at Division 1, 2, and 3 schools. For many of our scholarship players, college would not have been an option without MCW Starz.

MCW Starz (EIN 47-1852961) is a certified 501(c)(3) organization and a member of the national Amateur Athletic Union.
