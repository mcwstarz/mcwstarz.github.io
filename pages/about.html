Welcome to MCW Starz

Our Mission
Changing Lives On and Off the Court

MCW Starz has become a sought-after, elite AAU girls basketball program. We're committed to developing players both on and off the court, providing a foundation for success in basketball and life.

We offer competitive play at regional and national levels, with over 120 girls advancing to play in college and over 100 athletes earning spots on regional private high school basketball teams.

Our Impact
Beyond the Court: A Legacy of Excellence

National Circuit Recognition

MCW Starz competes at the highest level of amateur basketball. We are proud members of the nation's premier circuits, including Power 24, Select 40, and the Select Events Circuit.

National Accolades

Highlights include a prestigious Run for the Roses Championship and featured coverage on ESPN, bringing national visibility to our players and program.

Development Model

100% of our Class of 2024 development players who started with us in 5th/6th grade are now playing collegiate basketball. Our Open Division players are competing at DII and DIII schools nationwide.
