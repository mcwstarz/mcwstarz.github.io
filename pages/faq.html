About MCW Starz

MCW Starz is an elite AAU girls' basketball program that develops athletes through competitive teams, skill training, recruiting support, and holistic health and wellness programming.

MCW Starz was founded by Mandy Carter-Zegarowski, with support from her brother Michael Carter-Williams, NBA Rookie of the Year and professional basketball player.

The mission of MCW Starz is to develop confident, skilled, and resilient athletes by combining elite basketball training, leadership development, and long-term athlete support.

MCW Starz is open to girls in grades 5 through 12. Athletes are placed in teams and training groups based on age, skill level, and development needs.

MCW Starz combines elite coaching, structured player development, recruiting guidance, and health and wellness support to help athletes succeed both on and off the court.

MCW Starz has helped over 120 athletes continue their basketball careers at NCAA Division 1, Division 2, and Division 3 college programs.

MCW Starz is based in the Burlington, Massachusetts area, with training sessions and practices held at local partner facilities.

MCW Starz serves middle school and high school athletes, typically ranging from grades 5 through 12.

No. MCW Starz focuses on complete athlete development, including mindset, leadership, recovery, and long-term personal growth.

MCW Starz works with athletes at all levels, from beginners building fundamentals to advanced players preparing for college basketball.
